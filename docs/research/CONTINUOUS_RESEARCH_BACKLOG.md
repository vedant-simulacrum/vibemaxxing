## Research hygiene

- Prefer primary sources.
- Record source date and product/version scope.
- Separate facts, inference, recommendation, and accepted decision.
- Do not mark an item complete from documentation alone when an executable probe is required.
- Do not allow research reports to become a second source of truth; update canonical specifications and registers.
