# VibeMaxxing Product Scope Freeze

Updated: 2026-07-19
Status: accepted product direction; planning-only phase remains active

## Governing rule

The intended public launch is the complete initial product, not a narrow MVP. Internal delivery, private alpha, and beta may be staged, but staging may not silently reduce the launch target.

## Complete public-launch scope

### Competition

- global, friends, private-board, organization, hacker-house, and community leaderboards; country leaderboards are post-launch;
- daily, weekly, monthly, seasonal, yearly, and lifetime periods;
- Token Burn default ranking and explicitly Estimated Cash Burn;
- ties, movement, streaks, seasons, late/offline events, corrections, evidence filtering, quarantines, and archived standings.

### Social product

- public/private profiles;
- friendships, blocks, rivals, overtakes, and rank movement;
- active-session presence;
- notifications and user controls;
- private boards, organizations, hacker houses, communities, country boards;
- invitations, ownership, administration, moderation, appeals, and lifecycle behavior.

### Local product

- background daemon installed and controlled through CLI;
- privacy-separated collector and network sync processes;
- macOS menu-bar UX;
- Windows/Linux tray UX;
- headless CLI/daemon operation for WSL, containers, CI, and compatible remote environments;
- local permissions, adapter, device, privacy audit, outbound ledger, diagnostics, export, deletion, update, and rollback UX;
- hosted web dashboard linked to the local device without exposing transcript content.

### Agent compatibility

- all major agent families represented in a living machine-readable census;
- Hardened-certified, Competitive-certified, Community-certified, Generic live, Imported, and Unsupported states;
- representative commercial, open-source, local-model, CLI, IDE, desktop, cloud, orchestration, gateway, ACP, OpenTelemetry, and unknown-tool paths;
- generated support claims based on exercised conformance, not handwritten marketing lists.

### Identity and safety

- GitHub and X/Twitter OAuth primary sign-in;
- linked identities and optional passkeys/hardware credentials;
- sessions, recovery, provider loss/compromise, native authorization, export, and deletion;
- deterministic anti-cheat controls, evidence tiers, quarantine, moderator audit, appeals, restoration, and continuous red-team operations;
- privacy boundary preventing content-bearing data from leaving the device.

### Quality and operations

- complete route and state coverage;
- accessibility, responsive, browser, native-platform, visual, performance, battery, and reliability evidence;
- signed packages, notarization, TUF updater, rollback/freeze defense, SBOM/provenance, and consumer verification;
- deployment, SLOs, observability allowlists, backup/restore, disaster recovery, incident response, key compromise, moderation operations, legal/privacy readiness, and open-source governance.

## Staged internal delivery

Stages may validate one synthetic flow, one adapter, one platform, one ranking scope, or one social loop at a time. Such milestones are internal evidence only and must not be described as the complete public product.

## Public-launch gate

Launch requires the entire scope above, honest support-tier coverage across all major agent families, zero unresolved P0 contradictions, accepted residual risks, restored automated checks, complete privacy/threat traceability, production operations, public open-source readiness, and explicit user approval.
