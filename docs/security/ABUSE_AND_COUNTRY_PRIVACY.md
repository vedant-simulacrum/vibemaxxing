# Abuse Resistance and Country Privacy

## Principles

- Resist spam, replay, collusion, and mass account creation without making ordinary participation invasive.
- Country leaderboards are coarse community views, not identity verification.
- Enforcement must be explainable and appealable.

## Progressive controls

- Passkey-backed accounts and device registration
- Per-account, per-device, and network-aware rate limits
- Friend-request and board-creation throttles
- Account-age and activity-history weighting for high-impact actions
- Duplicate-device and suspicious-cluster detection
- Quarantine rather than silent deletion when evidence is uncertain
- Optional stronger verification only for prizes, official competitions, or exceptional abuse risk

## Country handling

- Derive a coarse country assertion from a short-lived trusted signal or user-confirmed suggestion.
- Store source category, confidence, effective date, and correction history.
- Do not publicly expose exact IP, city, or coordinates.
- Allow opt-out and correction.
- Suppress country slices below a minimum cohort threshold.
- Prevent rapid country switching from affecting active rankings.
- Never describe the result as nationality, citizenship, or verified residence.
